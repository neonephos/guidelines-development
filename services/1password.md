# 1Password

## Normative Language

The key words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **REQUIRED**, **SHALL**, **SHALL NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, **RECOMMENDED**, **MAY**, and **OPTIONAL** in this document are to be interpreted as described in [RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119.html) and [RFC 8174](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174.html).

## Summary
[1password](https://1password.com/) is a password manager that enables teams 
to securely store and share credentials, passwords and other confidential 
information. 

## Guidelines

* Projects **MAY** apply to 1Password's 
  [free open source plan](https://github.com/1Password/for-open-source).

**Limitations:**

* 1Password's free open source plan is limited to 10 users.
